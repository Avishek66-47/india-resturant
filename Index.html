Our Story

A culinary journey spanning from the majestic Himalayas to the beautiful sunny shores of Alcudia.

We established Indian Food Alcudia with a singular vision: to bring the true, uncompromised flavors of our heritage to Spain. We master classic Indian curries and smoke meats in traditional clay Tandoors, while proudly featuring authentic Nepalese influences crafted with unique, hand-blended mountain spices. Every ingredient is fresh, every spice is authentic, and every guest is treated like family.
